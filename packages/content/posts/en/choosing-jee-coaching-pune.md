Choose by what you can check, not by the advertisement. Before you pay any coaching class in Pune, check eight things. Who will teach? How big is the batch? How do tests work, and where do doubts go? Then check the travel time at class hours, the full fee, the refund rules and how results are shown.

## 1. Who will actually teach your child

Ask for the names of the Physics, Chemistry and Maths teachers, and how many years each has taught JEE. Then ask to meet them. A big name on a banner means little if a different teacher takes the class every week.

Ask one more question: how long have these teachers been at this centre? A teacher who leaves in November takes the plan for your child's year with them.

## 2. How many students are in the room

"Small batches" is not a number. Ask how many students sit in one class, and whether that number holds all year. In a room of 60, a quiet student can go months without being asked a question. In a room of 30, the teacher can see every notebook.

## 3. How tests are set, and what happens after

JEE Main is taken on a computer: {{fact:exam.jee-main#questions}} questions in {{fact:exam.jee-main#hours}} hours. Ask whether the mock tests run on a screen like the real exam, or on paper. Ask how often there is a test, and what your child gets back afterwards.

A test is only useful if someone reads it with your child. Marks alone do not say which chapter to go back to. A chapter-by-chapter analysis does.

## 4. Where doubts go

Every student gets stuck. Ask what happens next. Is there a set time for doubts? Can a student ask online after class? Can they sit with one teacher for a hard problem? If the answer is "they can ask in class", ask what happens when the class has moved on.

## 5. The travel time at class hours

Most JEE classes run in the afternoon or evening, when Pune roads are at their slowest. Make the trip once, on a weekday, at the time your child would travel. A centre that is 20 minutes away on Sunday morning can be 45 minutes away at 3 PM.

If you live in Aundh, Baner, Balewadi or Pashan, our [Pune page](page:pune) shows the route to each of our centres. For Wakad, Sangvi and the rest of [Pimpri-Chinchwad](page:pimpri-chinchwad), the area pages do the same.

## 6. The full fee, in writing

Ask for one written figure that covers everything for the whole course. Then ask what is not in it. Study material, the test series, online access and taxes are sometimes charged on top. Two quotes can only be compared once both include the same things.

## 7. The refund rules, before you pay

Ask what happens if your child has to leave the course: after one month, after six months, or because the family moves. Ask for the answer on paper. A coaching class that will not write down its refund rules is telling you something.

## 8. How results are shown

A topper's photo tells you little on its own. Ask which course the student took at this centre, and for how long. A student who joined for a two-week crash course is not the same as one who studied there for two years.
